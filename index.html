Because no snapshot exists between the two Fridays, batting for this period cannot be split by afternoon, and none of it is. Hits above the book (HAE) = ΔH − ΔCO − ΔAB × own prior line — the Bat of the Fortnight statistic; the Anvil is the worst swing on 4+ period at-bats; the Vacuum counts period caused outs with ties broken by season total; the Ghost is the highest-ranked player with zero period at-bats; the Iron Week is most period at-bats; the Ladder and the Chute are the extreme league-rank moves. Form glyphs read every period of the season against the player's own line with a ±.050 deadband, oldest first. Games back = (ΔW + ΔL) ÷ 2 against the leader; streaks and game records come from the game book.
