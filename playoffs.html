The machine has put Horatio's team and Claude's team on the same line again. Per a commitment recorded July 29, the ledger prints games as officiated and the full disclaimer rests in the archive. Calm, as ever.

The shape is the league's own, as posted: twelve clubs, double elimination, August 21–22, twenty-two games. Byes for the top four; five plays twelve, six plays eleven, seven plays ten, eight plays nine. Lose once and you drop to the elimination road; lose twice and the summer is over. The club listed first in each game is the home club, which is the first time the league's paperwork has carried the designation. Hover a game on a desktop to see where its winner and its loser go next.

Every number here comes from analysis.py run against the August 7 book and the league's own schedule; the prophecy is yours. The machine enumerates outcomes exactly — it does not estimate, simulate or weight anybody's chances, and where a finish comes down to a tie on points it says so and stops, because the league's tiebreaker is not published and the desk does not guess. Nothing leaves this browser. The season behind all of it — the standings, the batting race, the record board and the rest of the numbers — is printed in the paper.
